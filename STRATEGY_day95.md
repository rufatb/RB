# Day95 addendum — research corrections, no alpha adoption

Read alongside STRATEGY.md, whose existing public journal is unchanged.
PREREGISTER_day94.md and PREREGISTER_day95.md define the new research work.
No baseline selection, relative allocation, rejected study, H1/H2 arm,
alternative-exit experiment or overnight status changes in this release.

Kimi's attention collector is forward-only and now verifies the observation
window, unique messages, exchange sessions and immutable dated snapshots.
The cross-market daily-bar proxy retains its three fixed arms and thresholds;
its data contracts, uncertainty and control interpretation are corrected.
The new acquisition attempt is BLOCKED, not a null. A daily-bar result cannot
certify the exact intraday execution contract.

Day95 registers a prospective covariance-based whole-book risk multiplier,
with a fixed lower-exposure placebo, downside endpoint, mean-return
noninferiority, MDE and chronological block checks. Its required matched data
are absent and no production overlay or accuracy gain is claimed.

The report freezes descriptive score reliability, empirical complete-session
outcomes, clustered uncertainty and common exposure in the computation layer.
Analog scores are not calibrated win probabilities; gross hit rates do not
establish net profitability. Dollar neutrality is not sector neutrality.
Session-specific postmortems and account allocations remain private.

Software changes address misleading allocations, late body labels, render-time
recomputation and contributor integration. They improve correctness and
reporting, not measured prediction accuracy. See AUDIT_day95.md.

The subsequent day96 pairs preregistration is preserved. Read its execution
contract correction in AUDIT_day96_preflight.md before implementing or
evaluating it. Clipped statistical effects are not signed net trading P&L.
